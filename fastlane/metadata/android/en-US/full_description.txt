Resonance is a music player for self-hosted, Subsonic-compatible servers such as Navidrome. Connect to your server and stream your own library.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Stream music from any Subsonic or Navidrome server</li>
<li>Browse artists, albums, and playlists</li>
<li>A home screen of recently played, newest, most played, and random albums</li>
<li>Play, pause, skip, seek, shuffle, repeat, and a play queue</li>
<li>Background playback with a notification and lock-screen controls</li>
<li>Search artists, albums, and songs</li>
<li>Star songs and browse your favorites</li>
<li>Synced and plain-text lyrics with auto-scroll</li>
<li>Scrobble played tracks back to your server</li>
<li>Several color themes</li>
</ul>

Built with Jetpack Compose and Material Design 3.
